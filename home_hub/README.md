# Home Hub

A family energy dashboard for Home Assistant, in your sidebar.

- **Home:** live animated power flow, battery level, today hour by hour, a 7-day outlook and
  one-tap controls.
- **Solar & energy:** where every kWh went, solar yield against sunshine, when each panel string
  starts and stops making useful power, and self-powered days.
- **Appliances:** health scores that compare each appliance with its own normal, so you notice a
  fridge working harder before it fails.
- **Habits:** when the house uses power, an appliance clock, and the always-on load.
- **Weather & forecast:** temperature vs usage, and a 7-day prediction with the battery
  simulated hour by hour.
- **Savings plan:** steps to send back more than you use, simulated on your own hourly data.
- **Alerts:** raised automatically, tracked over time, and optionally pushed to your phone.

See the **Documentation** tab for options.
